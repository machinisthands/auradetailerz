FAQ

Common questions

Ceramic coating or PPF — which one do I need?

Ceramic coating adds gloss and makes the paint easy to clean. PPF is a physical film that protects against stone chips and scratches. Many owners combine both: PPF on high-impact areas, ceramic on the rest. We'll advise after inspecting your car.

Ceramic or graphene coating — what's the difference?

Both add gloss and a protective, water-repellent layer. Graphene coatings are a newer formulation known for strong water beading. We'll recommend the right one for your car and budget.

Do you detail bikes too?

Yes — from cruisers like the Royal Enfield Continental GT to superbikes like the KTM Duke. Foam wash, degreasing and coating are all available for bikes.

How long will my car be at the studio?

It depends on the service and your car's condition. Interior detailing is usually quicker; paint correction and coating jobs need more time for prep and curing. We'll give you a clear timeline when you book.
